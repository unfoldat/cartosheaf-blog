# Claim

"South Korean accessibility regulations require at least 30 cm of unobstructed space on both sides of tactile paving."

## Decomposition into verifiable units

1. Does any Korean statute, 시행령(enforcement decree), or 시행규칙(enforcement rule) contain a numeric "30cm" figure connected to tactile paving (점자블록)?
2. If a 30cm figure exists, what does it actually measure — the physical size of a single tactile block unit, the distance a warning block must be laid *in front of* a hazard, the lateral clearance on *both sides* of a paving strip, or something else?
3. Is there a distinct Korean requirement for an obstacle-free buffer zone running alongside (not in front of) tactile paving, and if so, what is its actual dimension?
4. Is whatever requirement exists a binding legal obligation (law / 시행령 / 시행규칙) or a non-binding design recommendation (지침 / 매뉴얼 / 해설서)?
5. Does the requirement, if any, apply specifically "on both sides," or only in one direction of travel?
6. Is there a plausible, evidenced explanation for how "30cm on both sides" could have arisen from the actual Korean regulatory text, if the claim turns out not to match it exactly?

Each unit above is checked against primary legal text first, then cross-checked against government/agency guidance, then design guidelines, standards, and disability-organization material.
